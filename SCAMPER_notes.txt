DEAR DATA 100 - SCAMPER NOTES
Topic: my daily steps and movement (105 observations, 5 Oct - 24 Nov 2026)
One observation = one walk or gym session, with steps, time, context, pace and weather.

SUBSTITUTE
- Replaced a plain chart or grid with a clock-face spiral.
- Replaced numbers on axes with shapes, colours and sizes.

COMBINE
- Combined time of day and date into one position: angle = time of day, distance from the centre = how recent the observation is.
- Combined weather (colour), steps (size) and context (shape) in a single mark.

ADAPT
- Adapted the idea of a clock face and tree rings, where the oldest data sits in the middle and newer data grows outwards.
- Adapted the Dear Data idea of a hand-made legend explaining how to read the picture.

MODIFY
- Made the size of each shape depend on step count, so big gym sessions stand out from short walks.
- Gave each context its own shape: squares for gym sessions, triangles for uni commutes, circles for other walks.
- Made rushed walks shake slightly, to show pace through movement.

PUT TO ANOTHER USE
- Used a clock, normally for telling time, to show the rhythm of my week (gym around midday, commutes in the morning).

ELIMINATE
- Removed numeric axes and gridlines; a short legend explains the encoding instead.
- Left out extra detail until the viewer hovers over a shape.

REVERSE / REARRANGE
- Put the oldest data at the centre and the newest at the edge, instead of a left-to-right timeline.

CODE (for the coding criterion)
- I wrote a class called Movement. Each row of the CSV becomes one Movement object, stored in an ArrayList.
- Each object stores its own data, works out its own position, size, colour and shape, and draws itself with display().
